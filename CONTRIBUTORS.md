# Thank you!

Sparky grew out of an APRS iGate inspired by [Ionosphere](https://github.com/cceremuga/ionosphere). Thanks to everyone who has helped improve it through code, review, documentation, testing, bug reports, ideas and radio experiments.

The [GitHub contributor history](https://github.com/oorrwullie/sparky/graphs/contributors) and [pull requests](https://github.com/oorrwullie/sparky/pulls?q=is%3Apr+is%3Aclosed) record contributions and reviews. They don't capture every way someone can help, so if you have a contribution that deserves acknowledgment, let us know. Please only add named acknowledgments with the person's consent.

We also owe a lot to the open-source tools and libraries that make Sparky possible, including rtl-sdr, multimon-ng, PortAudio, Go, Paho MQTT and Mochi MQTT. See [go.mod](go.mod) for Go dependencies and their upstream projects.

Even the person who points out a confusing sentence helps the next person get started. Thank you.
